Where the time went: features rebuilt for every model by hand, one model family at a time, and manual comparison in spreadsheets. The rebuilt pipeline prepares features once, trains candidates in parallel and writes the leaderboard itself.

Synthetic subscribers in Jabodetabek, generated in scripts/build_data.py; no operator or client data. The scorecard is fitted the usual way: fixed bins, weight of evidence per bin, logistic regression, 600 points at 50:1 odds and 20 points to double the odds. Code and data · Case study
